Three pillars, one score

Every layout is graded on a transparent, weighted composite. Tune the weights to match your priorities — the optimizer adapts.

Material flow
Minimize transport distance weighted by throughput. High-volume machine pairs are pulled adjacent so parts travel less between stations.
Worker safety
Enforce minimum aisle widths, exit clearances, heat separation between high-output machines, and explicit no-go zones — flagged as violations.
Equipment utilization
Reward compact, balanced layouts that respect every machine's clearance halo and leave room to work, without wasting floor space.

Note: GalvBot uses heuristic optimization (simulated annealing) and a transparent flow model. It is decision-support tooling — not a certified safety or structural engineering authority. Always validate against local codes.

How it works

1. Capture

Draw your floor to scale, or upload a blueprint/photo to auto-detect the boundary and obstacles.

2. Define

Place machines from the palette, set clearances, and wire up material-flow throughput between stations.

3. Optimize

Run simulated annealing in a web worker. Watch the score improve live, then apply or keep your own.

4. Export

Ship a PNG, the full project JSON, or a one-page PDF summary with the score breakdown and violations.
